# Data Dictionary

## companies.xlsx

Purpose: Master company information.

## profitandloss.xlsx

Purpose: Revenue, profit, expenses, EPS.

## balancesheet.xlsx

Purpose: Assets, liabilities, borrowings.

Purpose: Operating, investing and financing cash flows.

## sectors.xlsx

Purpose: Sector classification.

## stock_prices.xlsx

Purpose: Historical stock price data.

## market_cap.xlsx

Purpose: Valuation metrics.

## peer_groups.xlsx

Purpose: Industry peer mapping.
